- Global Settings

---

## 5. Best Practice Notes

- Keep the main ERP navigation to 12–15 root menus only.
- Put detailed operational items under submenus.
- Use role-based visibility to reduce clutter.
- Keep Platform Admin separate from business operations.
- Keep AI Intelligence as a dedicated module rather than mixing it into the core flow.

---

## 6. Final Recommendation

The final structure is best organized as:

Dashboard → Organization → Products → Customers → Suppliers → Sales → Procurement → Inventory → Logistics / Distribution → Finance → HR & Staff → Reports & Analytics → Administration → Documents → AI Intelligence

This keeps the menu clean, enterprise-ready, and suitable for both a trading ERP and a SaaS multi-tenant platform.
